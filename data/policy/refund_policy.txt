SUPPORTAI REFUND & RETURN POLICY
Version 2.1 | Effective Date: January 1, 2026
====================================================

1. ELIGIBILITY WINDOW
---------------------
- Physical products: Returns accepted within 30 days of confirmed delivery.
- Digital products / subscriptions: No physical return required; refund eligibility is
  30 days from purchase or charge date.
- Items must be unused and in original packaging UNLESS the item arrived damaged or
  was incorrect — in those cases, used or opened items are accepted for return.

2. REFUNDS
----------
- Full refund: Issued when the product is defective, damaged on arrival, incorrect
  (wrong item shipped), or when the customer cancels within the eligibility window
  and meets return conditions.
- Partial refund: May be issued at support agent discretion when the item has been
  partially used but a genuine defect exists. Typical range: 20%–50% of order value.
- Processing time: Refunds are credited to the original payment method within 5–7
  business days of approval.
- Duplicate charges: Automatically refunded within 2 business days upon verification.
- Cancelled-order charges: If a charge occurred on a successfully cancelled order,
  a full refund is issued immediately.

3. REPLACEMENTS
---------------
- Available within the 30-day return window for wrong items, defective items, or
  items damaged in transit.
- If an item arrives damaged/broken and the customer requests a replacement, the replacement is immediately approved without escalation, provided the item value is under $500.
- Customer must indicate preference for replacement over refund; if preference is
  unclear, default to offering refund.
- Replacement ships within 2 business days of approval; original item need not be
  returned for orders under $150. For orders $150 and above, a return label is sent.

4. INVOICE CORRECTIONS
-----------------------
- If the customer was charged an amount different from the agreed/listed price,
  a corrected invoice is issued and the price difference refunded within 5 business days.
- Invoice reissue requests (e.g., for tax/GST breakdown) are fulfilled within 1 business day.
- Corrected invoices are sent as PDF attachments to the customer's registered email.

5. SUBSCRIPTIONS
----------------
- Cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing cycle unless the
  customer requests immediate cancellation, in which case a pro-rated refund applies.
- If a charge occurs after a confirmed cancellation, it is refunded in full — no
  questions asked.

6. ESCALATION TRIGGERS (HUMAN REVIEW REQUIRED)
------------------------------------------------
The following situations must be escalated to a human agent:
  a. Order ID does not exist in the system.
  b. The email content is ambiguous or contradicts the order record.
  c. Claim value exceeds $500 and involves suspected fraud.
  d. Customer has had more than 3 refund requests in the past 90 days.
  e. Technical support issues (account access, app bugs, etc.) — these are not
     handled by the automated refund system.
  f. Warranty claims — route to the dedicated warranty team.
  g. Missing assembly instructions — route to product content team.

7. OUT-OF-SCOPE ITEMS
-----------------------
- This policy does not cover third-party seller products.
- Shipping delays caused by courier are not eligible for refunds under this policy
  unless the item has not arrived within 15 business days of estimated delivery.
- Cosmetic damage to packaging (not the product itself) does not qualify for a refund.

8. CONTACT & ESCALATION
------------------------
- Automated refunds and replacements are processed by SupportAI.
- Human escalation is handled by support@supportai.example.com within 24 hours.
- Emergency line (fraud/security): +1-800-SUPPORT (business hours only).
