TECHGADGETS INC. SUPPORT & RETURN POLICY
Version 3.0 | Effective: 2026

1. REFUNDS & RETURNS
--------------------
- Physical electronics: Returns accepted within 30 days of confirmed delivery.
- Items must be in original condition with all accessories.
- Full refunds issued for defective, damaged on arrival, or incorrect items.
- Duplicate charges are automatically refunded within 2 business days.

2. REPLACEMENTS
---------------
- Available within 30 days for wrong items, defective electronics, or transit damage.
- Replacements under $500 approved automatically. Replacement ships within 2 business days.
- Over $500 requires human manager review.

3. INVOICE & REISSUE
--------------------
- Corrected invoices issued within 1 business day for tax breakdown or pricing discrepancies.

4. ESCALATIONS & SECURITY
-------------------------
- Order ID not found in system must be escalated.
- Suspicious receipt images, font tampering, or serial number mismatches MUST BE ESCALATED.
- Customers with >3 refund claims in 90 days must be escalated.
