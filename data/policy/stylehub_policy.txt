STYLEHUB APPAREL SUPPORT & RETURN POLICY
Version 1.5 | Effective: 2026

1. CLOTHING & APPAREL RETURNS
------------------------------
- Apparel items: Returns and exchanges accepted within 14 days of delivery.
- Must be unwashed, unworn, with original tags attached.
- Store credit issued for size exchange requests.

2. DAMAGE & DEFECTS
-------------------
- Garments with manufacturing defects or stain on delivery eligible for full refund or replacement.
- Photos required for claims over $100.

3. ESCALATION RULES
-------------------
- Worn/washed clothing claims must be escalated.
- Claims without valid order number or receipt must be escalated.
- Suspected fraudulent receipts must be escalated immediately.
